Liquidity quoter

How this works

Posts post-only bids at the top of both sides of markets with Kalshi liquidity rewards. You get paid for resting orders even when they don't fill; the cost is being filled right before the price moves. Turn it on in Settings. Every quote expires on its own within ~2 minutes if the app stops.

How the estimate is made

Estimate = pool × your share under Kalshi's scoring (bids walked to Target Size, discounted below the reference price, both sides averaged) at today's book; 0 when the book is too thin for snapshots to count. Picked by estimated reward per $ of capital until the market count or the $ cap. Markets paying under Kalshi's $1 minimum are skipped. Re-picked every 10 minutes.
